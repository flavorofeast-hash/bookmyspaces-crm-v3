## Confirmed positioning boundaries

- Skyline: market as accommodation/stay only. Do not feature Skyline in event, wedding, birthday, or corporate-event marketing content.
- Monurama: market as accommodation + events, but never depict or promise event capacity above 100 guests total, or above 40–50 on the rooftop / 15 per hall.
